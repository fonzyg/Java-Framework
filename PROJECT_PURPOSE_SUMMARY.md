# 📋 Alfonso's Auto Parts Shop - Project Summary

## 🎯 What This Project Is Supposed To Do

### 🏢 **Business Purpose**
Alfonso's Auto Parts Shop is a **comprehensive inventory management system** for an automotive parts retail business. The system enables efficient management of auto parts inventory, product catalogs, and customer purchases through a professional web-based interface.

---

## 🚀 **Core Functionality**

### 1. **Inventory Management System**
- **Track Auto Parts**: Manage individual automotive components (engines, brake pads, oil filters, etc.)
- **Monitor Stock Levels**: Real-time inventory tracking with minimum/maximum thresholds
- **Price Management**: Handle pricing for all parts and products
- **Inventory Alerts**: Prevent stockouts and overstocking through validation rules

### 2. **Product Catalog Management**
- **Product Display**: Showcase assembled automotive products (engine kits, brake systems, etc.)
- **Product-Part Relationships**: Track which parts compose each product
- **Inventory Integration**: Link product availability to part inventory levels

### 3. **E-Commerce Functionality**
- **Buy Now Feature**: Allow customers to purchase products online
- **Real-Time Inventory Updates**: Automatically reduce inventory when purchases are made
- **Purchase Confirmation**: Provide feedback on successful transactions
- **Stock Validation**: Prevent sales of out-of-stock items

### 4. **Business Information Portal**
- **Company About Page**: Professional company information display
- **Service Offerings**: Detail available automotive services
- **Business Credibility**: Establish professional online presence

---
